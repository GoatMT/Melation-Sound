Artist Bio

Meet the artists and collaborators connected to A Broken Dream.

01 · ALBUM ARTIST

Osama

Osama is one half of the album artist credit behind A Broken Dream, bringing the record's dark, direct voice to the core tracklist.

02 · ALBUM ARTIST

MT

MT is the second name in the Osama, MT artist credit, helping shape the identity of A Broken Dream from its first track to its final outro.

03 · FEATURED ARTIST

Adam

Adam appears as a featured collaborator on “Nightmare Fuel,” adding another voice to the album's developing sequence.

04 · PRODUCER + BONUS ARTIST

Bassam

Bassam is credited as the producer on “Nightmare Fuel” and performs the available bonus track “Nawaf's Stole Pain.”
