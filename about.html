01 · THE SOUND

Leave the edges in.

Raw, direct, and atmospheric. Melation Sound makes room for heavy feelings, sharp details, and moments that do not need to be polished away.

02 · THE CATALOG

One release at a time.

The catalog begins with A Broken Dream, a developing album by Osama and MT, alongside the private MT single 10:20. New music gets its own page, story, and place in the archive.

03 · THE PEOPLE

Artists stay visible.

Osama, MT, Adam, and Bassam are not hidden behind a label name. The Artists page shows how each voice, collaborator, and producer connects to the releases.

Every available song can be streamed free on the website. Open a song page for its artwork, lyrics, credits, metadata, and dedicated player.
